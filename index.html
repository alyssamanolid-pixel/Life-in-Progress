ABOUT LIFE IN PROGRESS

No perfect
timeline required.

Life in Progress is a digital space for young adults who are learning how to do life as they go.

There is no universal timeline for graduating, getting a job, saving money, falling in love, moving out, or figuring yourself out.

This is a space for practical resources, honest reminders, and small tools that make the messy parts of growing up feel a little more manageable.
